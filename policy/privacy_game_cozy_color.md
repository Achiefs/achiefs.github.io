This Privacy Policy describes what information is collected, used, and shared when you
play **Cozy Color** (the “Game”).

Last updated: November 20, 2026.

**PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT**

We **do not collect personal information ourselves**: there are no accounts, no sign-up and
no analytics of our own. We run no servers, so nothing you do in the Game is sent to us. We
do **not sell** personal information.

Some versions may show ads or accept donations, always through third parties, as described
below.

**DATA THAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE**

The Game saves on your own device, and only there, what it needs so you can carry on
painting where you left off:

- Your painting: which drawings you have started or finished, the colours you have chosen
  for each piece and your brush strokes.
- Your settings: whether free drawing mode is on.
- If a future version records a donation, the mark that remembers it, so you are never
  asked twice.

None of that is sent anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

**DONATIONS AND ADS**

The Game is complete without paying anything, and playing it never depends on donating or on
watching an ad. Some versions may still offer **optional donations** and **advertising** as
a way to support its development.

- **Donations** are processed entirely by the store or payment provider shown at checkout,
  for example Google Play. We never see or store your card or billing details: we only
  receive confirmation that a donation was made. The information the payment provider
  collects is governed by its own privacy policy, such as
  [Google's](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Ads**, when shown, are supplied by a third-party advertising provider. That provider may
  collect device information and identifiers to serve and measure ads, as described in its
  own privacy policy. We do not receive or keep that information, and we do not use it to
  build profiles of you.
- Neither donations nor ads require an account, and you can keep painting without using
  either.

**PERMISSIONS**

This version of the Game **asks for no permissions** at all: no location, no contacts, no
camera, no storage, not even internet access to play.

If a future version shows ads, the app will declare internet access, used only to load
them.

**CHILDREN**

The Game itself collects no information, so we collect no information from children either.
If ads are shown, their provider may collect the data described above; we configure them to
respect the age-related rules that apply.

**CHANGES**

We may update this privacy policy from time to time for personal, operational, legal, or
regulatory reasons.
