This Privacy Policy describes what information is collected, used, and shared when you
play **Sopa de Letras** (the “Game”).

Last updated: October 15, 2026.

**PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT**

The Game **collects no personal information at all**: there are no accounts, no sign-up, no
advertising, no analytics and no trackers. We run no servers, so nothing you do in the Game
travels anywhere.

**DATA THAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE**

The Game saves on your own device, and only there, what it needs so you can carry on
playing where you left off:

- Your progress: the levels you have beaten, your stars, your best times and the hints you
  have left.
- Your settings: the language.
- If you have bought the endless hints, the mark that says you own them, so you are never
  charged twice.

None of that is sent anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

**PURCHASES**

Hints are bought **through Google Play**, which is the party that handles payment data: the
Game never sees or keeps your card or your billing details. All the Game receives is the
confirmation of what was bought, and it uses it only to hand over the hints. The information
Google collects during a purchase is governed by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The Game is played **offline**: the purchase is the only thing that uses the network.

**PERMISSIONS**

The Game **asks for no permissions** at all: no location, no contacts, no camera, no
storage, not even internet access to play.

**CHILDREN**

Since no information is collected, no information is collected from children either.

**CHANGES**

We may update this privacy policy from time to time for personal, operational, legal, or
regulatory reasons.
